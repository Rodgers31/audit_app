with external/provider network denied and bounded resources:

1. Freeze source/corpus/artifact/runtime/schema/role identities and control witnesses.
   Seed nonempty preimage fixtures, unrelated sentinel rows and representative
   later-write conflicts. Apply the **reviewed candidate fixture delta**, then the
   exact proposed reversal on that fixture. Record both transactions and row/view/
   lineage recapture; require conflict refusal without mutation. This does not

2. Prove original-row/value/qualified-public behavior recovery, unrelated-row

   and migration compatibility. Require missing/corrupt preimage, stale expected
   row, interrupted transaction and replay/idempotency controls. An empty fixture
   or returning exit0 without recapture is incomplete.
3. Execute rollback artifact/runtime locally on isolated loopback with OFF controls;
   prove startup/health/private auth and rendering of qualified/held values. Preserve
   original artifact bits and cache identities. Compare actual controls, not just
   launch variables. Measure cleanup and exact process/container/schema absence.
4. Bind full commands, environments, test/JUnit/phase inventories, actual exits and
   cleanup to final bytes and independent review. Missing target/cache capability
